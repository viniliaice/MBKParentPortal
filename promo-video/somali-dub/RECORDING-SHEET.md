# Warqadda Duubista — Codka Af-Soomaali (Awdal)
## Recording sheet — MBK Parent Portal promo

**Waxa loo baahan yahay / What is needed:** 6 duubis oo gaaban — 6 short
recordings. Waqtiga guud waa ku dhowaad 40 ilbiriqsi oo cod ah.
Total spoken audio: about 40 seconds.

**Yaa duubaya / Who records:** cod nin Soomaali ah oo qaangaar ah, oo af-Soomaali
Awdal (Boorama) ku hadla. A mature male Somali voice, native to Awdal/Borama.

---

## Sida loo duubo — How to record

1. **Meel aamusnaan ah.** Qol aan wax sawaxan ah lahayn — ha jirin baabuur,
   TV, qaboojiye ama dabayl.
   *A quiet room. No traffic, TV, fridge hum or wind.*

2. **Telefoonkaagu waa ku filan yahay.** Voice memo / Voice Recorder app.
   Telefoonka miiska saar, ha ku qaban gacanta (si aan gariir u imaan).
   *Your phone is fine. Rest it on a table, don't hold it — handling noise
   ruins the take.*

3. **Telefoonka afkaaga ka fogee 15–20 cm.** Ha ku dhawaanin aad — haddii kale
   "p" iyo "b" ayaa qarxa.
   *Keep the phone 15–20 cm from your mouth, slightly off-axis so plosives
   don't pop.*

4. **Ha ku duubin muusig hoostiisa.** Cod kaliya — anaga ayaa muusiga ku darnaa.
   *Voice only, no music underneath. We add the music.*

5. **Mid kasta si gaar ah u duub.** Fayl kasta hal sadar ha ka bilaabo.
   *Record each line as its own file, starting on the words — no long silence
   at the head.*

6. **2–3 jeer ku celi mid kasta.** Kadib kan ugu fiican dooro.
   *Do 2–3 takes of each; we'll pick the best.*

## Tilmaanta guud ee codka — Overall voice direction

- **Cod deggan, diirran, oo miisaan leh.** Ha ahaan mid degdeg ah ama mid
  xayaysiin qaab-ciyaareed leh.
  *Calm, warm, weighted. Not rushed, not "salesy".*

- **Codkaaga hoose isticmaal.** Laabta ka hadal, sanku ha ka hadlin.
  *Speak from the chest, not the nose.*

- **Xuruufta `x`, `c`, `q` si cad u dhawaaq.** Tusaale: `maqnaan`,
  `xaadirinta`, `casharrada`, `xaqiiji`. Ha jilcin.
  *Articulate the pharyngeals clearly. Don't soften them — this is what makes
  it sound Awdal rather than generic.*

- **Shibbanayaasha laba-laab ah ilaali:** `warbixinnada`, `casharrada`,
  `ardaydana`.
  *Preserve the geminates.*

- **Ha akhrin sida qoraal — u sheeg sida hadal.** Sida aad waalid la hadlayso.
  *Don't read it like text — say it like you're speaking to a parent.*

## Sadarka 1aad — Line 1
**Fiidyowga: 0–12 ilbiriqsi · Waqtiga ugu badan: 8 ilbiriqsi**

> ### Waalid, ilmahaaga maalintiisa dugsiga ha ka maqnaan.
> ### MBK Parent Portal ayaa wararka dugsiga meel keliya kuu keenaya.

*Tilmaame:* Ku bilow cod aad u deggan. `Waalid` — si toos ah u dheh, sida aad
qof la hadlayso, oo joojin yar ka dib samee. Sadarka labaad wax yar ka dhaqso
gelin, oo kalsooni ku dheh.

*Direction:* Open very calm. Say `Waalid` directly, as if addressing someone,
then leave a small beat. Second sentence slightly more forward, with quiet
authority. This is the hook.

---

## Sadarka 2aad — Line 2
**Fiidyowga: 12–21 ilbiriqsi · Waqtiga ugu badan: 6.5 ilbiriqsi**

> ### La soco dhibcaha iyo warbixinnada, xaqiiji xaadirinta,
> ### shaqada gurigana indhaha ku hay.

*Tilmaame:* Saddex qaybood oo isku xigta — sida aad wax tirinayso. Qayb kasta
wax yar kala saar. `indhaha ku hay` — diirran ku dhammee.

*Direction:* Three clauses in a row, like counting them off. Separate each
slightly. Land `indhaha ku hay` warmly.

---

## Sadarka 3aad — Line 3
**Fiidyowga: 21–28 ilbiriqsi · Waqtiga ugu badan: 4.5 ilbiriqsi**

> ### Fariimaha iyo ogeysiisyada dugsiga hal meel ka akhri.

*Tilmaame:* Kan ugu gaaban, kan ugu deggan. Hal hadal oo nadiif ah. Muusiga ha
neefsado hoostiisa.

*Direction:* The shortest and calmest line. One clean statement, slightly
softer than line 2. Let the music breathe underneath.

---

## Sadarka 4aad — Line 4
**Fiidyowga: 28–35 ilbiriqsi · Waqtiga ugu badan: 6.5 ilbiriqsi**

> ### Ardaydana casharrada, layliyada iyo imtixaannada fasalka
> ### ayaa waxbarashada sii wadaya.

*Tilmaame:* **Halkan codka kor u qaad.** Waa halka ugu muhiimsan. Sharaf iyo
faan yar ku dheh. `sii wadaya` — wax yar hore u riix, sida heesaa ereyga ugu
dambeeya u jiido. Ha degdegin.

*Direction:* **Lift here — this is the emotional peak.** A touch of pride and
resonance. Push `sii wadaya` slightly forward, the way a singer leans into the
last word of a phrase. Don't rush it.

---

## Sadarka 5aad — Line 5
**Fiidyowga: 35–41 ilbiriqsi · Waqtiga ugu badan: 4.5 ilbiriqsi**

> ### MBK Parent Portal — ku gal iimaylka uu dugsigu kuu diiwaangeliyay.

*Tilmaame:* Dib u deg. Magaca barnaamijka marka hore, joojin cad, kadib
casuumaad diirran — amar maaha. Si nadiif ah u dhammee, neef ha ku dambayn.

*Direction:* Settle back down. Brand name, a clear beat, then the instruction
as a warm invitation rather than a command. End clean, no trailing breath.

---

## Sadarka 6aad — Line 6 (fiidyowga gaaban / 15 s social cut)
**Fiidyowga: 0–15 ilbiriqsi · Waqtiga ugu badan: 14 ilbiriqsi**

> ### Ilmahaaga maalintiisa dugsiga ha ka maqnaan. Dhibcaha, xaadirinta,
> ### shaqada guriga iyo fariimaha hal meel ka arag. Casharrada iyo
> ### imtixaannada fasalkana raac. MBK Parent Portal — ku gal iimaylka
> ### laguu diiwaangeliyay.

*Tilmaame:* Midkan hal mar oo isku xigta. Ka dhaqso badan kan dheer, laakiin
ha goyn — diirran sii ahaada. Sadarka ugu dambeeya (magaca) wax yar kala saar.

*Direction:* One continuous take. Faster than the long cut but never clipped —
still warm. Separate the final brand line slightly.

## Marka duubistu dhammaato — Once recorded

Faylasha halkan soo dhig, ama noo soo dir. Kadib:

```bash
cd promo-video
python3 scripts/generate_somali_voice.py file --from-dir /path/to/takes
node scripts/build-somali-dub.mjs measure   # xaqiiji waqtiga / timing QC
node scripts/build-somali-dub.mjs all       # labada fiidyow / both cuts
```

| --- | --- |

| `somali-dub/short-version-somali-awdal.m4a` | Codka kaliya + muusig |

Muuqaalka fiidyowga waxba kama beddelanno — pixel kasta waa sidii asalka.
*The picture is never re-encoded — every frame stays byte-identical to the
original master.*
