## Who we are

The MBK Parent Portal is provided by [school name, address, contact] for the
families of its pupils. The school decides what is recorded about a pupil; the app
simply shows it to that pupil's parent or guardian.

## What the app handles, and why

This list is taken from what the app actually reads and writes — not from a
generic template:

| Data | Where it comes from | Why the app needs it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parent/guardian name, email, phone numbers | Provided by the school when the family account is created; the parent can be asked to confirm | Sign-in, identifying the right family account, and letting teachers and the office reach the family |
| Child's name, class, enrolment | School records | Showing the parent the right child and the right class |
| Marks and assessments | Entered by teachers | The results the parent opens the app for |
| Attendance | Entered by teachers | Attendance history |
| Homework | Entered by teachers | Homework list and due dates |
| Report comments | Entered by teachers and the principal | Term reports |
| Messages between the parent and the school | Written in the app by the parent; replies come from the school | Parent–teacher communication |
| Announcements for the child's class | Written by the school | School notices |
| Quiz answers and learning progress | Written when the child takes a quiz or a lesson in the app | Results history and progress |
| Device push token | Generated by the phone when notifications are enabled | Delivering notifications about the above |
| Sign-in session | Held on the phone after signing in | Keeping the parent signed in without retyping a password |

The app does **not** collect: location, contacts, photos, files, calendar, call
logs, advertising identifiers or analytics profiles. It shows no advertising and
uses no third-party tracking or profiling.

## Who can see it

- **A parent or guardian** sees their own children's records and their own
  messages. The database enforces this per family; it is not just a screen
  arrangement.
- **School staff** see what their role requires: teachers the classes they teach,
  supervisors and the office the school's records, administrators the school.
  A teacher does not see another class's families' messages.
- **The service providers** that run the system process it on the school's
  instructions: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage) and Expo/
  Firebase (delivery of push notifications). They do not use it for their own
  purposes.
- **Nobody else.** There is no data sharing, selling or advertising.

## Children's data

The app is used by parents and guardians, not by children. The records it shows
concern children, and are visible only to that child's own family and the school
staff who need them. Children's records are kept as part of the school's own
record-keeping.

## Keeping it, and deleting it

- **Account data** — sign-in, name, email, phone numbers, messages, learning
  progress, quiz attempts, push token — is deleted when the family deletes the
  account. The app has this built in: Account → Delete account. The same
  explanation and the web resource required by Google Play are described in
  `docs/account-deletion.md`.
- **School records** — enrolment, attendance, homework and marks — are kept by the
  school after the account is deleted, and are no longer linked to the family
  account. A family may ask the school to correct or remove them.
- **Push tokens** are removed when notifications are turned off or the parent
  signs out.

## Security

Access is controlled in the database itself, per family and per role, not only in
the app. Sign-in is required: nothing about a child is readable without an
authenticated session. Session data is stored in the app's private storage on the
phone, and device backup of that storage is switched off in this app's Android
configuration.
